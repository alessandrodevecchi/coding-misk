# Design system

Retro-futuristic synthwave by default, with a per-visual accent palette and an optional hardware theme. All styles live in `src/style.css`; tokens are CSS custom properties on `:root`.

## Tokens

Base (Sunset look): `--bg #0d0818`, `--panel #160f26`, `--panel2 #1f1534`, `--line #3a2a5c`, `--ink #f3ecff`, `--muted #a597c7`, `--a1 #ff4fa3` (primary accent), `--a2 #ffb347` (secondary accent). Step colors of the 909-style sequencer: `--s1 #ff4f6d`, `--s2 #ff8a3d`, `--s3 #ffd23d`, `--s4 #f4f0ff` (one color per beat group).

Each visual sets `data-look` on `<html>` and overrides the tokens:

## Typography

- Logo: "coding" in `Mr Dafoe` (neon script, white with `--a2` glow) over "MISK" in `Russo One` (chrome gradient, skewed), on a perspective grid, with an 8-bar equalizer driven by the instruments.
- UI: `Chakra Petch`. Code, numbers, readouts: `JetBrains Mono` (the code font is a setting, see Code themes). Hardware LCD: `VT323`.
- Labels (`.lbl`): 11px, uppercase, letter-spacing .14em.

## Components

- `.card` panels, `.btn` (primary uses `--a1`), `.chip` toggles, `.tab`, `.led` channel switch, `.ramp` automation toggle (↗), `.arr-scene-btn` scene blocks (width proportional to bars, `.playing`, `[aria-current]`), `.timeline` with `.sec` blocks and `.head` playhead, `.step` sequencer keys (drum grid and `.chsteps` rows in melodic channels), arranger grid (`.arr-strip` sections and `.arr-grid` rows share one CSS grid template: a `--trk-col` label column, then `minmax(54px, <bars>fr)` per section; `.trk-row` with `.trk-head` name, `.mini` mute/solo keys, `.trk-cell` cells: `.on` pattern clip, `.custom` striped partial clips, `*` marks per-section overrides), `.track-panel` (header, pattern chips, `.tp-editor` with drum grid, `.chsteps` step row, `.notegrid` degree grid, code textarea; `.ctrl.over` marks a setting overridden in the selected section), rack (`.device` cards with `.led` bypass, `.dev-head` code preview, `.dev-args`), section view (`.srack` with `.scard` compact cards, `.hits` read-only rhythm), full section view (one `.tp-host.tp-full` panel per playing track, `[aria-current]` on the selected one), player ruler (`.arr-ruler`: `.ruler-time` current / total, `.ruler-track` slider with bar numbers, `.ruler-fill` and `.ruler-knob`; positions map through the section blocks, so it works in both views), free timeline (`.tl-mode` on the arranger: gapless proportional section strip, `.trk-lane` with bar ticks and `.sec-line` section dividers, `.clip` blocks positioned in % of the song with a `.clip-grip` resize edge, `.clip.free` for `start`/`bars` clips), sound browser (`.sb-top` search and view chips, `.sb-cats`, `.sb-groups` with pixel icons, `.sb-cards` / `.sb-list` / `.sb-pads` results, `.sb-insp` inspector with `.sb-photo` and `.sb-credit`; `img.px` is pixel art scaled with `image-rendering: pixelated`), code panel (`.code-resize` grip up to 50% width, `.code-collapsed` rail, `.codecol.full` overlay).
- Player bar (`.pbar`, fixed at the bottom, 52 px, the page keeps that much bottom padding): `.pb-btn` round icon keys, the `.play` key, `.pb-now` with `.pb-onair` (red when lit), `.pb-title` and `.pb-pos`, `.pb-vol` volume with mute; the same volume next to the radio controls (`.radio-vol`). On phones the position and the previous key are hidden and ON AIR becomes a dot.
- Guide (`.guide`, `#48`): `.g-index` chips, `.g-card` per feature with a `.g-mode` badge, `.g-row` label and text pairs (What it does, How to use it, Good to know), `.g-sub` sub-sections, `.g-scope` table, `.g-b` button names and `kbd` keys in text, `.g-flash` on the control "Show me" points to, `.g-help` round "?" next to each tab's intro.
- Radio (`.radio`): `.radio-grid` controls and now playing card, `.onair` light, `.ctrls.four` sliders on two rows in a narrow panel (container query), `.mat-source` chips for the material source under the sliders, `.radio-curve` energy line. Curves of the song on air (`.curves`, `#40`, look `#47`): energy on top as the main curve (grid, part names, `.charge` zone), detail lanes (`.radio-lane[data-lane]`, one colour each) folded under `.curves-toggle`; small squares hollow when automatic and filled when set, a glow on set parts, the past shaded, a `.curve-tip` box with every value on hover or while dragging. HW theme: each curve in a dark `.screen`, amber traces, set parts warm white, the position green. Console (`.radio-steer`): `.steer-group` blocks of `.steer-btn` keys; the Parts group has a `.steer-part` row of chips (lead, bass, arp, pad) above the Vary and Swap keys, which carry drawn icons (`VARY`, `SWAP` in `src/icons.js`, `.with-ico`); a disabled key keeps its reason in the tooltip.
- Patterns tab (`#72`): `.pn-top` search and source chips, `.pn-kinds` chips, `.pn-group` sections by kind (`.pn-ghead`), `.pn-cards` of `.pn-card` (a round `.pn-cplay` key, `.pn-cbody` with name, kind and a `canvas.pn-pv` preview: notes against time or a step grid in `--a1`, the current step in `--a2`, the note playing in `--ink`), `.pn-fresh` strip for new parts not saved (dashed `--a2` border), `.pn-detail` card on the right (stacked first on a narrow tab, container query) with `.pn-ctrls`, `.pn-styles`, `.pn-actions`, `.pn-codebox` and the `.pn-gen` row. Icons are drawn SVG (`src/icons.js`: `PLAY`, `STOP`, `LISTEN`, `KEEP`, `PENCIL`, `ADD_TO`). In Compose, `.pt-presetrow` puts the listen key next to a preset menu; in a style sheet, presets are `.tag-chip.pat-link` buttons.
- Toasts for feedback; two-tap confirmation instead of `confirm()`.

## Navigation

Two modes at the top of the work area (`#42`): **Listen** (Compose, Songs, Playlists, Radio) to make and hear music, and **Groove Lab** (Artists, Styles, Genres, Sounds, Patterns, Guide, References) for the material music is made of and for learning. The mode buttons are large toggles with the tab names as a subtitle (hidden on phones); in the hardware theme they are latching keys with a lamp. Under them, the tabs of the mode. Each mode remembers its last tab; a link to a tab of the other mode switches mode. The live code stays on the right in both modes and can be collapsed; the settings open from the gear next to the code, with no mode lit.

## Code themes (`code-themes`)

The code panel and the code in videos have their own theme, chosen in Settings → Appearance (`src/code-themes/`). Automatic follows the app theme: NEON gives **Neon**, built from the look's tokens (ground `--bg` darkened by a quarter, text `--ink`, label and variable `--a1`, string `--a2`, number `--s3`, comment `--muted`), HW gives **Amber** (ground `#140e04`, text `#ffb347`, like the LCD fields, a soft glow). Also **Terminal** (`#0b0d10`, the modern palette of the Terminal render), **High contrast dark** (black, white, yellow labels) and **Light high contrast** (white, black, purple labels, dark blue strings), the latter used by the light app theme (`data-ui="light"` maps to it). Every text role reaches 4.5:1 on its ground, 7:1 in the two high contrast themes, Neon on every look (`npm run check:code-themes`). A few of Strudel's own themes are offered under their Strudel names.

Code font: JetBrains Mono by default (Share Tech Mono, VT323, the system monospace), 13, 14 or 16 px, one step larger in the full view. The panel's ground (`--code-bg`), the flash (`--code-flash`), the dimmed widget's outline (`--code-muted`), the widgets, the playing-note outline and the text of muted lines (55 %) follow the theme.

## Light theme (`data-ui="light"`, `light-theme`)

The third button, LIGHT (it: CHIARO), next to NEON and HW in the top bar and in Settings → Appearance. A light variant of neon in `src/theme-light.css`: same layout and fonts, ground `#f5f3f8`, panels white, `--panel2 #efecf4`, dividers `--line #d4cde0`, ink `#15111d` (at least 7:1), muted `#574e66`, and one accent pair per look picked by hand to reach 4.5:1 on every ground (palco `#b0105a`/`#00687a`, studio `#9a4f00`/`#b8101f`, edgerunners `#6b5f00`/`#00687a`, pixel `#855c00`/`#b0183c`, montagne `#00657f`/`#5a3fd0`, spazio `#5a42d6`/`#a3157c`, sonar `#006b47`/`#4a6800`). `color-scheme: light`, so selects and scrollbars turn light. Borders of controls (inputs, buttons, chips: `--ctl-line`, `--chip-line`) reach 3:1; dividers stay faint.

- **The stage stays dark.** Inside `.stagewrap` the tokens go back to neon's of the look (`--dk-*`), so the tempo line, the look menu, full screen, the soul's keys and the typed comment stay light on the dark stage; the stage gets a soft frame. The looks and the stage widgets draw exactly as in neon (the stage's `theme()` stays `neon`).
- **Device screens keep their dark glass:** the LCD readouts (`.comp-lcd`), the Soul display's shell, tab and cable, the sound pads and knobs, the sound pictures.
- **Shared colours are tokens** with neon's values in `:root` (`--chip-bg`, `--danger`, `--rec`, `--info`, `--charge`, `--on-accent`); LIGHT gives them light values. Code outside the editor (`pre`, a pattern's code) takes the code theme's ground; the radio's lanes, the FM lab's drawings and the timeline read the light tokens.
- **Videos, fragments and the live output do not change:** they read the `--dk-*` tokens when present, and so does the Neon code theme. With Automatic the code panel is Light high contrast.
- **Follow the system** (`coding-misk-ui-follow`, off by default): LIGHT or NEON from `prefers-color-scheme`, live; pressing a theme turns it off. A script in `index.html` sets `data-ui="light"` before the app starts, so a reload does not flash dark.
- Checks: `npm run check:light-theme` (every token pair), `tools/check-light-theme.cjs` (every tab in LIGHT: text against its computed background, dark patches, dark canvases; with `--theme neon|hw --static --compare` the pictures against an earlier run).

## Hardware theme (`data-ui="hw"`)

Switch NEON/HW in the top bar (`coding-misk-ui`). Anodized panels with screws, rubber keys, amber LCD fields (`--lcd-bg #1b1306`, `--lcd-fg #ffb347`, `VT323`), knobs replacing range inputs (arc in `--a1`, pointer cap rotating -135° to +135°), power LEDs on channel switches, green activity LEDs (`--led #39ff6a`) driven by each channel's level, bezels around the stage and the code editor. Knobs drive the hidden original inputs, so keyboard use and state stay the same. The player bar becomes a brushed panel with square mechanical keys (they sink when pressed), an amber display for title and position, a small volume knob and a round red ON AIR lamp.

## Visuals (`src/visuals.js`)

Each theme reacts to per-instrument levels and onsets (kick echoes and flashes, hats sparkles, snare glitch, guitar shake, pad and hook glows, riser sparks) and draws the summed waveform.

- **Palco (Stage):** cyberpunk stage laid out in units: hi-hat, kick, snare and crash (lit by FX), bass cabinet, guitar amp stack with an electric guitar, keyboard and arp sequencer, lead synth with oscilloscope, FX sampler pads, Tesla coil (riser). LED wall with the waveform, light beams, skyline. Labels on one row.
- **Pixel:** 200px-wide buffer scaled up: dithered sky, banded sun, low-poly mountains, city with lit windows, road, bouncing car.
- **Tramonto:** synthwave sun, perspective grid, waveform on the horizon.
- **Montagne:** wireframe ridges reacting to bass and guitar, moon with echoes.
- **Spazio:** nebulae, warp starfield, waveform aurora, rising ringed gas giant, moon, comets, retro ship.
- **Sonar:** radar sweep per bar, echo rings, blips.
- **Studio** (`#28`, the radio's look by default): acoustic foam wall, an ON AIR light lit while the radio is on air, a microphone whose ring lights up and sends waves while the voice speaks, a screen with the song, artist, styles, bar and the last spoken comment typed out, a radio dial whose needle finds the station, two VU meters, a desk with one fader per instrument and a turntable that spins at the song's tempo. A look picked while the radio plays becomes the radio's look.
- **Edgerunners:** halftone moon, layered city with cyan rim light and holo signs, elevated train, anime speed lines, glitch slices with RGB split, cyberware HUD.
- **Monitor** (`widgets-on-stage`, `#79`): a wall of up to 6 panels, one per playing instrument, on a dark gradient. Each panel: the instrument's name in its colour and the widget kind in the label colour (JetBrains Mono 600), a level bar top right, the code widget (scope, pianoroll, punchcard, spectrum as a scrolling grid of 24 bands, spiral, pitchwheel), a border that glows with the level and a short flash on each onset; a silent instrument is grey, see-through, with a dashed border. Grid by the stage's proportions: 1 panel large and centred, 2 side by side (stacked on a tall stage), 3 as two over one centred (a column on a tall stage), 4 as 2×2, 5 as three over two, 6 as 3×2 (2×3 on a tall stage); the top leaves room for the readout and the look buttons.
- **Stage widgets layer** (widgets-on-stage D4): Monitor's panels over another look, the panel background at 60% opacity so the scene shows through, the playing look's colours by default. Strip: two per side around the middle on a wide stage (about a fifth of the width and height each), a centred row at the bottom on a tall one (2×2 for 4). Palco: inside the Palco look's LED wall, up to 4, fewer bigger. A panel's level bar shortens rather than run over its header on a narrow panel.
- **Rullo** (en: Roll): the melodic instruments on one pitch axis (the section's range, at least an octave) around a playhead in the middle, 4 bars across, each in its colour with a legend top right; past notes half lit, the note sounding lit with a glow; kick, snare and hats as lanes of dots at the bottom, the one just hit bigger, the lane name on a dark plate.
- **Widget colours** (`src/visuals/widget-palettes.js`): neon: kick `#ff2e88`, snare and bass `#00e5ff`, hats and guitar `#ffe14d`, arp `#3dffb0`, pad `#a98bff`, lead `#ff7ad9`, fx `#ff9f43`; hardware: kick `#ff5a3d`, bass `#ffb347`, snare `#ffd27a`, hats `#ffe2a8`, guitar `#ffcf4d`, arp `#39ff6a`, pad `#d9b77a`, lead `#ff9a5c`, fx `#ff8c42`, on the amber LCD tones with a softer glow.

- **Soul** (`#46`, `src/soul/`): the song playing as a retro science-fiction screen, ported from the v3 mockups in `docs/soul/v3`. Ten views drawn on an offscreen canvas of 1200×760 logical pixels and fitted into the stage, the free sides filled with a blurred, darkened copy. A Tracker, B Terrain, C Sphere are CRT screens (green phosphor `#5dff8a`/`#b8ffcb`, amber `#ffb347`/`#ffe2a8` in HW, scanlines and vignette drawn in the canvas). D Lattice, E Strands, F Landscape, G Particles, H Halftone, I Aura, J Spectrum are colour views whose hue follows the last other visual plus a seeded shift; in HW they pass through a retro filter (400×254, five amber levels, 4×4 Bayer dither, scanlines) with the caption redrawn readable. Fonts: VT323 and Share Tech Mono on the CRT screens, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono on the colour views. Overlays: an "analyzing" line typed with a progress bar on a new song, an interference with a "recalibrating" line on steering. Labels use the app's own names (`MISK/OS 6000`), never film trademarks. At most 30 frames a second; fewer points on phones.

**Soul display** (`#46` phase 2, an easter egg): a small screw in the stage's bottom right corner (three clicks) or typing "soul" lets a "SONG SOUL ANALYZER" tab peek out of the stage's right edge (blinking OPEN, back in after 20 seconds); OPEN opens a bay at the stage's right and the screen first asks "ANALYZE NOW?" (VT323 in the shell's phosphor, YES and NO boxes, text variants); the stage shrinks and grows a little in height, the bay's doors open (neon: cyan rim and yellow door edges; HW: hazard stripes), the display comes out and switches on. Seven shells seen from the front, from `docs/soul/display-v4` (MISK/OS 6000 the HW default, MiskVision the neon default); a selector on the bay's bottom edge (two arrow keys, then a small display with the number and the name). Override runs a SCART cable from a socket under the bay to one under the stage (neon: rubber with signal pulses; HW: braided with one lamp), drawn as a rope that hangs, swings and can be dragged.

The visual picker is a dropdown next to the full-screen button; with Soul, the view buttons (previous, name, next, lock) sit under it. Soul keeps the app accents of the last other visual (`data-look`). The stage area has a CRT scanline overlay, off for Soul, which draws its own; visuals respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
