# Song soul, v2

The second generation of the song soul views (`#46`), after the owner's notes on v1 (`../v1`). Same ten views and the same two test songs, with more life and more difference between songs. Everything a song does differently comes from its seed; small events come and go in time.

- **A Tracker:** each ring wobbles with its own seeded shape and trembles; now and then an arc glitches, a patch of the radar turns to big pixels, ghost contacts flicker, and the sweep lags for a moment ("SIGNAL LAG").
- **B Terrain:** unchanged.
- **C Sphere:** a less perfect globe: spikes where a curve is very high (they breathe and sometimes shoot out), craters where it is very low, seeded lumps; meridians flash, two points link with a dashed line, the rotation lags now and then.
- **D Lattice:** nodes flash on the beat, a pulse runs along edges, extra nodes grow out of the faces and fade, the colour shifts with the song.
- **E Strands:** floor tiles glow softly, floor lines flicker or redraw, dust lights drift, the strands pulse with a soft glow.
- **F Landscape, G Particles:** colours shift with the song.
- **H Halftone:** each song has its own ripple shape (circle, square, diamond, hexagon, star), wave centre and warp; drops fall at random, each with its shape; colours shift with the song.
- **I Aura:** each song has its own number of lobes, stretch and twist; coloured strands and fog drift along the edges.
- **J Spectrum:** random soft pulses in the columns, geometric glows (rectangle, triangle, diamond), the energy line glitches now and then.

In the neon theme the colour views could take their hue from the radio's visual (`?hue=` forces it); in the HW theme the retro amber filter stays as in v1. Open `soul-v2.html` with the same parameters as v1.
