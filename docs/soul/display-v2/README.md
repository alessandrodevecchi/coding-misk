# Soul display, v2

Second round of the Soul display shells for `#46` phase 2, after the owner's notes on v1 (`../display-v1`): the v1 shells did not convince, except A and D; more versions from the owner's references, then keep the most interesting. Open `display-v2.html` (self-contained, the real soul renderer runs on every screen; NEON or HW and the view at the top right).

- **Twelve shells:**
  - S1 MISK/OS 6000 (Nostromo console, v1 A redone);
  - S2 Unified terminal (wasteland terminal with a hood and a keyboard);
  - S3 Wrist-Link (wrist computer with a gauge, a wheel and a lamp);
  - S4 Shelter terminal (cream terminal with ribs and a floppy slot);
  - S5 Desk terminal (compact steel terminal with two knobs);
  - S6 Deck console (starship console with pill keys);
  - S7 Bridge display (starship desk display on a sculpted base);
  - S8 Viewer 1966 (classic starship viewer with data cards);
  - S9 MiskVision (2000s CRT, v1 D redone);
  - S10 Pro monitor (broadcast monitor with a button panel);
  - S11 Home computer 1958 (mid-century TV computer);
  - S12 Flat panel 2002 (early LCD on a round stand).
- The wasteland family (S2 to S5) keeps a monochrome screen: green, amber in HW.
- **Shell switcher**, instead of the side lever: a horizontal lever on the bay's bottom edge, one button that cycles with a flip counter and a name window, or a rotary selector.
- **Cable:** thick, with an old-school plug (SCART or a round connector) into a socket on the stage's edge. Neon: rubber with a glowing ring and signal pulses. HW: braided, no glow, one small lamp on the plug.

`compare-neon.jpg` and `compare-hw.jpg` show the whole page in both themes.
