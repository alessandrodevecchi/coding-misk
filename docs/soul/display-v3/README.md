# Soul display, v3

Third round of the Soul display shells for `#46` phase 2, after the owner's notes on v2 (`../display-v2`): seen from the front, no perspective or shadows, no keyboards, straps or legs; the display and its box fill the bay as if set into the interface; the references inspire, they are not copied; fewer shells, more detail and realism. Open `display-v3.html` (self-contained, the real soul renderer on every screen; NEON or HW and the view at the top right).

Every shell carries the display's own controls, and they work in the page: power, full screen, previous and next view, lock and override.

- **A · MISK/OS 6000:** industrial sci-fi console in cream painted metal, square keys with lamps, a guarded override toggle.
- **B · Unified terminal:** wasteland terminal in rusted olive steel, a hood around the tube, bakelite view knobs, a red power button, bat toggles for hold and patch to stage. Monochrome screen.
- **C · Wrist-Link:** wrist computer panel in worn gunmetal, a signal gauge with a live needle, a big view knob, rubber keys, a tuning wheel for the override. Monochrome screen.
- **D · Deck console:** starship console in satin silver, a colour frame printed around the picture, black pill keys that light their ring.
- **E · MiskVision:** 2000s CRT monitor in silver plastic, front buttons, blue power lamp.
- **F · Pro monitor:** broadcast monitor in grey metal, a button panel, and a red tally lamp on top that lights when the display overrides the stage.
- **G · Electronic Brain 1958:** mid-century two-tone cabinet, an "ON AIR" meter, piano keys, a chrome source dial (display or stage) for the override.

Monochrome screens are green in the neon theme and amber in HW. `compare-neon.jpg` and `compare-hw.jpg` (with override on) show the whole page. The shell switcher and the cable are still the v2 proposals.
