# Soul display, v4

The Soul display in the app's top area, after the owner's notes on v3 (`../display-v3`): the shells of v3 with more detail, a shell selector, the SCART cable in place, and a hidden way in. Open `display-v4.html`; "▶ Demo" at the bottom right plays the whole script with a visible cursor. `?demo=1&t=12` freezes the script at a time, `?state=open&shell=C`, `?state=override&hw=1`, `?sel=1` show states.

- **Hidden way in:** no button on the stage. A small screw in the stage's bottom right corner looks like decoration; three clicks unscrew it, it drops and the hatch opens. Typing "soul" on the keyboard does the same. Power off on the display puts it back in the bay, and the screw screws itself back in.
- **Display:** fills the bay, seen from the front; the seven v3 shells, with the display's own controls (power, full screen, previous and next view, lock, override).
- **Shell selector** on the bay's bottom edge: SHELL ◀ and ▶ keys with a small display showing the number and the name, and one dot per shell (selector 2, the default). Selector 1 is a slide switch with detents, no lever (`?sel=1`, or the "Selector" button).
- **SCART cable:** a socket slides out of the stage's bottom edge (SOUL IN) and one from the bay's bottom edge (OUT). With override on, the plug leaves the display's socket, swings down under the stage and climbs into SOUL IN; the label on the plug runs vertically. Neon: rubber cable with a glowing ring, signal pulses and sparks. HW: braided cable, one small lamp on the plug.

`frames-screw.png` shows the screw being unscrewed and the hatch opening; `still-*.jpg` show override in both themes and the slide switch. Videos stay local in `notes/soul/display-v4/`.
