# Proposal

## Why

For variety the owner wants small random tools (#50): a random artist, a random genre or style for the radio, random knobs, and fresh instruments, each kept, redone or thrown away.

## What Changes

- **Dice buttons** (a drawn die icon, not an emoji):
  - in the radio, next to the artist, the styles and each knob, plus one for all knobs with a small "random / in range" switch;
  - a "sounds" die that draws new instruments and drum kits within the same styles for the next song;
  - in the Artists tab, "Random artist";
  - in the Styles and Genres tabs, play a random one.
- **Random artist:** name, pixel portrait, short bio, favourite styles, knob ranges, shapes, moves and quirks, all drawn from existing parts; Keep saves it among the listener's artists (editable), Again draws another, Discard drops it.
- **Only existing styles and genres** are picked; inventing new styles is a later upgrade (#51).
- **Seeds:** each result carries a small seed shown in its tooltip, so it can be found again.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `random-tools`: dice for artists, styles, genres, knobs and instruments.

## Impact

- Radio panel, Artists, Styles and Genres tabs, the artist format (a random artist is a normal artist), the director's instrument choice (a sounds seed), i18n, the Guide.
