## Why

Fragments (`#57`) work, but every one carries the same tagline, the tagline appears at once, and the texts for sharing lack the fields an upload form asks for. The owner approved a pool of short phrases, a typing effect, and descriptions that mention what inspired a song.

## What Changes

- **Tagline die.** A die beside the tagline puts another phrase from an approved pool into the field at each click; an arrow brings the default back. Agents can also ask for one phrase per fragment.
- **Typed tagline.** The tagline types itself in with a blinking block cursor and brief glitches, then stays.
- **More voice in the texts.** The approved "devotion" lines join the description moods, and two of them join the glitch messages.
- **Song inspiration.** Songs can carry an `inspiration` note (English and Italian); descriptions mention it with their own wording.
- **Upload fields.** The texts panel adds tags, hashtags and a category, with copy buttons, and the saved text file includes them.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `endless/vertical-clips`: tagline pool, random mode and the typed tagline.
- `endless/share-texts`: devotion moods, inspiration, tags, hashtags and category.

## Impact

- Fragment settings and menu, the vertical composer, the text generator and texts panel, the song format (one optional field) and a few built-in songs.
